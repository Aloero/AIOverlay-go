in tests/ui/package.json and fetched separately by npm ci. They and

packages downloaded for development.

sing-box v1.14.1
============================================================
Bundled as an unmodified, separately executed networking runtime from the
official SagerNet sing-box Windows amd64 release. AIOverlay does not enable
sing-box TUN or system-proxy integration; it uses an authenticated loopback
proxy owned by the application.

Project: https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box
License notice: runtime/sing-box/LICENSE
The bundled libcronet.dll is part of the official sing-box release archive.
